Stexx is for gay, bi and queer men to meet each other, without ads, without selling anyone's data, and without needing a real name. That only works if people treat each other decently. These guidelines apply to everything on Stexx: profiles, photos, messages and how you treat people you meet through the app. Breaking them can get content removed, an account hidden, or an account banned. They're part of our [Terms of Service](terms.md).

## Adults only

- **You must be 18 or older.** No exceptions, whatever the law is where you are.
- **No minors, ever.** Never post, request or share anything that sexualizes a minor, and never try to meet someone who might be under 18. Child sexual abuse material gets the account banned immediately, and we report it to the authorities.
- If you think someone is under 18, report them with the reason **May be under 18**. These reports go to the top of the queue and can hide the account straight away, before a moderator has looked.

## Your profile

- **Photos of you, and no explicit photos.** Your profile photos must be of you. No visible genitals, anus, or sexual acts on your profile. Shirtless and underwear photos are fine. Our photo check labels explicit profile photos automatically and hides them from everyone else; if you think it got one wrong, [contact support].
- **Be yourself, not someone else.** No fake profiles, no impersonating anyone (including Stexx staff), no stolen or stock photos. Your nickname doesn't have to be your real name, and we'll never ask for it.
- **No ads.** Don't use your profile to promote businesses, other apps, social accounts for followers, or paid content.
- **No hate.** No slurs, and nothing that attacks people for their race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, HIV status, gender identity, body or age. Writing your own preferences respectfully is fine; putting others down for who they are isn't.

## In chat

- **Consent first.** Don't send explicit photos to someone who hasn't said they want them. Respect "no", and respect silence.
- **Respect blocks.** If someone blocks you, don't contact them again, from this account or another one.
- **No harassment.** No threats, intimidation, stalking, unwanted repeated messages, or outing anyone. Never threaten to share someone's photos or messages.
- **What's shared with you stays with you.** Don't share anyone's photos, messages or personal details outside the chat without their consent. Expiring and view-once photos are meant to disappear; screenshotting them to share is a serious violation.
- **No spam or scams.** No mass messages, links to other services, requests for money, gift cards or crypto, or "verification" scams. New accounts can't send links and can only start a few new chats a day.

## Illegal and dangerous things

- **No sex work or solicitation.** No offering, asking for or advertising sex for money or anything of value, and no promoting escort services.
- **No selling or supplying drugs**, and no other illegal goods or services.
- **No violence.** No threats of violence, no encouraging self-harm, nothing that promotes violence against anyone.
- **No exploitation.** No human trafficking, blackmail or sextortion. If someone threatens to publish your photos unless you pay, don't pay; report them and block them.
- **Nothing illegal** where you are or where the other person is.

## Reporting

Reporting is quick, anonymous and free.

1. On a profile or in a chat, tap **•••** → **Report**.
2. Pick a reason: May be under 18, Harassment or hate, Spam or scam, Fake or impersonation, Threats or violence, Illegal content, or Something else. Add details if you like.
3. From a chat you can tick up to 20 messages, photos included, to show a moderator. Only what you tick is shared, even in end-to-end encrypted chats; nothing else from the conversation is.
4. **Also block** them is switched on by default.

The person you report is never told who reported them, how many people did, or why. Reports from several independent people hide an account from everyone until a moderator reviews it; an under-18 report from an established account does it on its own. Moderators then dismiss the reports, ask for a change, or ban the account. Banned accounts can't sign in again, and the phone they used can't make a new account. ## Blocking

- On a profile or in a chat, tap **•••** → **Block** and confirm.
- Blocking works both ways and right away: neither of you can see the other's profile, distance or messages, or find the other in Nearby, and they can't message you. They aren't told you blocked them.
- You can see and undo your blocks in You → Settings → Blocked members.

## What happens when guidelines are broken

Depending on how serious it is: the content is hidden or removed, the account is hidden until review, the account is limited, or the account and its phone are banned. Anything involving minors, sexual exploitation or credible threats of violence leads to a ban, and where the law requires it we report it to the authorities.
